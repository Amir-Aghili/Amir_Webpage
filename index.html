Intro

What I am all about.

I'm an Advanced Analytics Manager at Commonwealth Bank in Sydney, working across data science and machine learning. Before that I earned a PhD in Computational Mechanics, and I bring the same mathematical rigour to the models I build today.

I care about models that can be trusted, not just models that score well. On LinkedIn I write about the practical habits behind that, such as putting confidence intervals around metrics and controlling false discovery when screening features.

Working with: Python, machine learning, deep learning, data visualisation, Azure, Jupyter
